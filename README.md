# ATP005 — Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)

Preparation scaffold: one stage per exam domain, connected through `path.yaml`
to a training series repository. New material is planned, and the course
registration in `astrona-content` stays **draft**.

This exam is part of the current Golden Kubestronaut requirements.

| Stage | Domain | Weight | Series |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STG001 | Storage | 10% | [ATS022](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS022) |
| STG002 | Troubleshooting | 30% | [ATS023](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS023) |
| STG003 | Workloads & Scheduling | 15% | [ATS024](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS024) |
| STG004 | Cluster Architecture, Installation & Configuration | 25% | [ATS025](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS025) |
| STG005 | Services & Networking | 20% | [ATS026](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS026) |

Source: [official CKA curriculum](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka/), checked 2026-10-10.
Registration: `astrona-content/courses/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka.yaml`.
